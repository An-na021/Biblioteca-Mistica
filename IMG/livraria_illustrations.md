# Nossa livraria — ilustrações

Arquivo: `livraria_illustrations.png`.
Gerado com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, com fundo transparente.
Atlas de três células iguais: luminária, estante e plantas. O jogo usa `AtlasTexture` para exibir cada desenho; os textos, abas e botões são controles funcionais.

## Prompt final

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent game UI sprite sheet, landscape 3:1. The supplied image is STYLE REFERENCE only: match its small hand drawn dark sepia pen and pencil crosshatched illustrations. Create ONE sprite atlas with THREE equal square cells in one horizontal row, each centered with generous clear margins, all on genuinely transparent background: LEFT an antique glass oil reading lamp with flame and round base; CENTER a small rustic wooden bookshelf with several books, slight three-quarter perspective; RIGHT a grouping of three small potted leafy plants and flowers. Each illustration must stay completely inside its own third of the canvas, with no strokes crossing cell boundaries. Fine but clearly legible dark brown ink lines, vintage book marginalia, subtle hatching, no colored fill, no white fill, transparent interiors and background. No text, no labels, no frames, no book, no paper, no shadows outside the ink sketches. Keep all three subjects similar visual scale and centered.
