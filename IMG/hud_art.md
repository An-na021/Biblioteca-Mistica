# Shop HUD artwork

The dialogue parchment, status strip and wooden pause sign were prepared with the built-in image generation tool from `Biblioteca Mágica com Professor Bigodes.png`. All text and values are live Godot labels. AtlasTexture regions trim transparent padding without modifying the PNG files.

## dialogue

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Input: user reference of a fantasy bookshop with hand-drawn parchment HUD. Asset type: reusable blank Godot UI background, no baked-in text. Preserve the warm sepia ink, cream paper fibers, subtle edge aging, irregular torn edges, believable metal pins and small shadows from the reference. True transparent background outside the requested object. Remove all letters, words, numbers and decorative text. Do not include the shop or any character. Isolate and recreate ONLY the wide low dialogue parchment banner in the TOP LEFT of the reference. Make it a very wide horizontal cutout, approximately 5:1 width to height, tightly framed around the silhouette. Cream paper rectangle with a trailing folded ribbon tail to the right and two dark silver pushpins at upper left and upper right. Keep a small attached blank paper ticket protruding at the left, but REMOVE the paw symbol. Main central area should be completely blank pale parchment with no lines, no emblems, no title and no text. No other objects.
